<p>
  In 1993, roughly <strong>half</strong> of India's population had access to
  electricity. By 2024, that figure was <strong>99.9%</strong> — one of the
  largest electrification drives any country has completed. The curve is a
  reminder that the hardest part of infrastructure is often the last mile: the
  final villages, the last households, the connection that reaches the home
  rather than just the feeder.
</p>

<p>
  The acceleration came from <strong>Saubhagya</strong> (household
  electrification), the rural electrification programmes before it, and the
  parallel build-out of generation and grid capacity. Universal access is also
  the precondition for everything digital: a phone that charges, a clinic that
  runs, a data centre that operates.
</p>
