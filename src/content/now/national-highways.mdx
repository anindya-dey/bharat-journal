<p>
  India's national highways are less than 2% of its roads but carry around
  <strong>40%</strong> of its road traffic. In 1971 the network was under
  24,000 km; by 2024 it had crossed <strong>151,000 km</strong> — a six-fold
  expansion, most of it in the two decades since the
  <strong>Golden Quadrilateral</strong> and the National Highway Development
  Programme connected the four metros with four-to-six-lane highways.
</p>

<p>
  Roads and digital networks grew together: expressways,
  economic corridors, and four-laning that cut travel times and freight costs
  across the country.
</p>
