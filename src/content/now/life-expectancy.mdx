<p>
  At independence, an Indian could expect to live to about <strong>41</strong>.
  Today the figure is over <strong>70</strong> — roughly three extra decades
  added within a single lifetime. Few countries of India's size and starting
  point have achieved a larger health gain.
</p>

<p>
  The drivers were cumulative: the eradication of smallpox and polio, the
  expansion of primary healthcare and vaccination, better nutrition through the
  Green and White Revolutions, cleaner water, and — in the last decade —
  schemes like [Ayushman Bharat](https://pmjay.gov.in/) and a vast telemedicine network. The next
  frontier is not just length of life but quality of it, which is where
  AI-assisted and preventive care come in.
</p>
