<p>
  India's ports moved about <strong>9 million TEU</strong> of containers in
  2010; by 2024 that had risen to nearly <strong>24 million</strong>. Behind the
  numbers is a quiet transformation: the <strong>Sagarmala</strong> programme
  modernised ports and linked them to inland logistics, the Jawaharlal Nehru
  Port became one of the busiest container hubs in South Asia, and new
  deep-water terminals and dedicated freight corridors are moving cargo faster.
</p>

<p>
  Ports are where the export story is written — and where the next chapter
  (green shipping fuels, transshipment hubs) will be decided.
</p>
