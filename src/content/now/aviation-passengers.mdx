<p>
  India is now one of the world's largest and fastest-growing aviation markets.
  Passenger traffic grew from <strong>162 million</strong> in 2011–12 to over
  <strong>420 million</strong> by 2025–26, with a brief pandemic dip that was
  recovered within two years. A rising middle class, low-cost carriers, and the
  <strong>UDAN</strong> regional-connectivity scheme — which put small towns on
  the aviation map — drove the growth.
</p>

<p>
  The build-out continues: new airports, expanded terminals, and a fleet
  order book among the largest in the world, positioning India as a global
  aviation hub.
</p>
