<p>
  Aadhaar — a 12-digit digital identity — is the foundation of India's digital public infrastructure. The first Aadhaar was
  issued in <strong>2010</strong>; by 2018 the system held over
  <strong>1.22 billion</strong> identities, and by 2024 more than
  <strong>1.38 billion</strong>. No country had ever attempted identity at this
  scale, and none had done it so cheaply.
</p>

<p>
  It matters as an <em>enabler</em>: paired with Jan Dhan accounts and
  mobile numbers — the JAM trinity — it lets welfare reach the right person
  directly, lets a bank verify a customer in seconds, and lets [UPI](https://www.npci.org.in/what-we-do/upi) move money
  between strangers. Identity is the layer everything else authenticates
  against.
</p>

<p class="text-sm text-fog italic">
  Enrollment figures are UIDAI dashboard totals as reported; the chart shows the
  sourced milestone points (2010 launch, July 2018, October 2024).
</p>
