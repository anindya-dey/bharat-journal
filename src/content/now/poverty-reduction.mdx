<p>
  In 1977, <strong>59.7%</strong> of Indians lived below the international
  extreme-poverty line; by 1993 it was still <strong>47.5%</strong>. By 2023
  that had fallen to <strong>2.6%</strong> — and
  the World Bank estimates that roughly <strong>171 million people</strong>
  moved out of extreme poverty between 2011 and 2023 alone. No country has
  lifted more people out of poverty in a comparable period.
</p>

<p>
  The drivers were the compounding effects of growth after liberalisation, the
  Green and White Revolutions, and — critically for the last mile — the
  <strong>JAM trinity</strong> of [Jan Dhan](https://pmjdy.gov.in/) accounts, [Aadhaar](https://uidai.gov.in/) and mobile that
  carried welfare directly to the poorest. Poverty reduction is the ground on
  which every other ambition on this site stands.
</p>
