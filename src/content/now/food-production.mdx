<p>
  In the 1960s India imported food aid. Today it feeds well over a billion people and exports
  foodgrain. The <strong>Green Revolution</strong> brought high-yield wheat and
  rice; the <strong>White Revolution</strong> ([Operation Flood](https://www.nddb.coop/)) built the
  cooperative dairy system that made India the world's largest milk producer.
  The food production index has nearly <strong>tripled</strong> since 1990.
</p>

<p>
  Food security made the other achievements possible: a population that
  is fed can be educated, employed and lifted out of poverty. The next frontier
  is resilience — water security, climate-proof agriculture, and food systems
  that hold up as the climate shifts.
</p>
