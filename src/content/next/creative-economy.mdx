## The Spark

Korea turned pop culture into a national export; Japan and the US built
gaming and film empires; India already has the world's largest film industry by
output and a gaming market growing faster than almost anywhere. The stories are
global; the value capture is not.

## The Gap

India's creative output is enormous but fragmented and under-monetised. The
opportunity is to build the studios, animation and VFX houses, game studios and
platforms that keep the value — and the IP — in India.

## The Precedent

- **Indian cinema** releases more films than any other country, and Indian
  stories now reach global streaming audiences.
- **Animation and VFX** studios in India already do world-class work for
  Hollywood.
- **India's gaming market** is among the fastest-growing in the world, with a
  huge young player base.
- **Korea's K-culture** model shows how deliberate policy turns culture into
  exports.

## The Proposal

1. **A creative-industries mission** — incentives for studios, animation/VFX,
   game development and music, modelled on successful export programmes.
2. **Build original IP** — back Indian story-worlds, games and franchises for
   global markets, not just outsourced work.
3. **Creative-tech skills** — animation, game design and virtual production in
   the education system.
4. **Infrastructure** — world-class sound stages, virtual-production facilities
   and a global content marketplace.

Ask: the Ministry of Information & Broadcasting and MeitY to publish a
creative-economy roadmap with export targets.
