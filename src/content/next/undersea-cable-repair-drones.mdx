## Where I saw it

Almost all of India's international internet traffic travels through
undersea fibre-optic cables, and when one is damaged — by a ship's
anchor, a natural event, or deliberate sabotage — repair depends on a
small global fleet of specialist cable-repair ships, with wait times that
can run into weeks. No country has yet fielded autonomous underwater
drones capable of locating and assisting repairs faster than this
ship-dependent model allows.

## What's missing

India's digital economy depends entirely on these cables staying intact,
yet India has no dedicated, rapid-response repair capability of its own —
it depends on the same thin global fleet every other country shares.
Given India's coastline and the number of major cable landing stations it
hosts, a faster, autonomous response capability is both a resilience and
a sovereignty question.

## What India already has

- **Several major international cable landing stations**, making India a
  significant node in the global undersea cable network already.
- **The Deep Ocean Mission's underwater robotics and engineering base**,
  directly relevant to building repair-capable autonomous vehicles.
- **The undersea-anti-submarine UUV programme already proposed**,
  sharing much of the same underwater autonomy technology.

## The proposal

1. **A dedicated cable-inspection and repair-assist drone fleet**, based
   at India's major landing stations, to cut response time from weeks to
   days for damage within India's own waters.
2. **Share underwater-autonomy research with the UUV and deep-ocean
   programmes already proposed**, rather than building this as an
   isolated capability.
3. **A rapid-response protocol with telecom operators**, so a damaged
   cable triggers an immediate, coordinated drone deployment.
4. **Contribute data to global cable-security monitoring efforts**,
   building India's standing as a capable partner in protecting shared
   undersea infrastructure.

Ask: the Department of Telecommunications and the National Security
Council Secretariat to fund a dedicated undersea cable-repair drone
capability at India's major cable landing stations.
