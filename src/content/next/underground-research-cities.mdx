## Where I saw it

Deep-underground laboratories already exist around the world for a single
purpose each — neutrino detectors shielded from cosmic rays, seed vaults,
nuclear-waste research sites. What no country has built yet is a larger,
multi-purpose underground research settlement: housing, power, labs and
life-support together, functioning as a small city rather than a single
bunker or lab.

## What's missing

India has serious underground science already — deep mines repurposed for
neutrino and dark-matter research — but nothing at the scale of a
genuinely self-sufficient underground research settlement that could
double as a contingency site for critical national research if a surface
facility were ever compromised.

## What India already has

- **The India-based Neutrino Observatory and existing deep-mine
  laboratories**, proof India already builds serious deep-underground
  science infrastructure.
- **DAE's deep geological and radiation-shielding expertise**, directly
  relevant to building and operating a larger underground facility
  safely.
- **India's mountain and peninsular geology**, offering multiple stable
  sites to choose from.

## The proposal

1. **A feasibility study for one multi-purpose underground research
   settlement**, combining labs, power and limited habitation, not a
   single-instrument facility.
2. **Design explicitly for contingency use** — a working backup site for
   critical research programmes, not purely a novelty.
3. **Start from existing deep-mine science infrastructure**, extending it
   rather than starting a new site from scratch.
4. **Open parts of it to international scientific collaboration**, the
   way existing deep-underground labs already host shared experiments.

Ask: DST, DAE and DRDO to jointly commission a feasibility study for a
multi-purpose underground research settlement built on existing deep-mine
science infrastructure.
