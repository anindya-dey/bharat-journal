## Where I saw it

Commercial drone delivery in China and the US now runs thousands of
flights a day for food, medicine and retail parcels in specific cities,
and drone-based infrastructure inspection — pipelines, power lines,
bridges — has become routine in several countries rather than a novelty.
India's own PM Kisan Drone scheme already proved a single civilian use
case can scale fast once committed to.

## What's missing

India's 2021 Drone Rules opened the airspace and sparked a real domestic
drone-manufacturing industry, but most civilian use beyond agriculture
remains pilot-stage — medical delivery to remote areas, infrastructure
inspection, and disaster-response mapping each have scattered trials
rather than everyday deployment.

## What India already has

- **Drone Rules 2021**, already liberalising Indian airspace for
  commercial drone operations.
- **PM Kisan Drones (Namo Drone Didi)**, proof a single use case can scale
  nationally once backed properly.
- **A fast-growing domestic drone-manufacturing base**, already exporting
  and competing on cost.

## The proposal

1. **Scale medical and emergency-supply drone corridors** to every
   hard-to-reach health centre, not just demonstration routes.
2. **Mandate drone-based inspection** for a defined set of critical
   infrastructure — transmission lines, major bridges, pipelines —
   replacing manual inspection where drones are proven safer and cheaper.
3. **A disaster-response drone fleet** pre-positioned by state disaster
   authorities, ready to map and assess damage within hours, not days.
4. **Simplify beyond-visual-line-of-sight approval** for proven,
   lower-risk use cases, so pilots become standing operations faster.

Ask: the Ministry of Civil Aviation and DGCA to publish a scale-up plan
moving medical, inspection and disaster-response drone use from pilot to
standard operation.
