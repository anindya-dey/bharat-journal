## The Spark

AI can already read a retina, spot TB on a chest X-ray, and flag early signs
of cancer with specialist-level accuracy — in seconds, on a phone. In a
country where the nearest specialist may be hours away, that capability is
transformative.

## The Gap

India has built extraordinary healthcare delivery infrastructure — but the
specialist-to-population ratio remains a challenge, especially in rural
areas. The opportunity is to put AI-assisted screening at the *front* of the
system, so patients are triaged accurately and reach the right care sooner.

## The Precedent

- **eSanjeevani** is the world's largest government telemedicine network,
  with hundreds of millions of consultations.
- **Ayushman Bharat** covers hundreds of millions of people for secondary and
  tertiary care.
- **CoWIN** proved India can run a digital health platform at unprecedented
  scale.
- Indian research groups are already building validated **AI diagnostic
  models** for TB, diabetic retinopathy and oral cancer.

## The Proposal

1. **AI-assisted screening at every PHC** — validated models for TB, diabetic
   retinopathy, cervical and oral cancer, and anaemia, run on low-cost,
   portable devices.
2. **AI triage inside telemedicine** — so an eSanjeevani consult starts with a
   structured, AI-assisted assessment.
3. **A national validation pathway** — a fast, rigorous route for Indian AI
   health tools to be clinically validated and deployed.
4. **Data protection first** — consent, anonymisation and security standards
   baked in, with community health workers trained as the human layer.

Ask: the Ministry of Health, with AIIMS and ICMR, to pilot AI screening in
primary care across a few districts.
