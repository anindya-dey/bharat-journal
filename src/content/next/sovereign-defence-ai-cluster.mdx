## Where I saw it

As AI models move into command-and-control, intelligence analysis and
autonomous-systems targeting, several militaries have begun separating
defence-grade AI compute entirely from commercial cloud infrastructure —
air-gapped clusters that train and run models without ever touching a
network a commercial provider or foreign actor could access. It is a
distinct problem from civilian AI compute, with different threat models
entirely.

## What's missing

India's National AI Mission is building public compute capacity for
research, startups and government services — valuable, but explicitly
not designed for classified defence workloads. The quantum-radar-sensing
and next-generation-air-combat proposals already in this journal — the
mission systems, sensor fusion and swarm-coordination AI they call for —
all need a defence-dedicated compute base that doesn't exist yet.

## What India already has

- **The National AI Mission's public GPU compute procurement**, proving
  India can fund and operate large AI compute clusters.
- **DRDO's existing classified research-network infrastructure**, the
  security model an air-gapped cluster would extend.
- **India's growing base of foundation-model research talent**, already
  training large models for civilian applications.

## The proposal

1. **A dedicated, air-gapped AI compute cluster for defence research**,
   funded and operated separately from the civilian National AI Mission's
   infrastructure.
2. **Priority workloads**: command-and-control decision support,
   autonomous-systems training for drone swarms, and intelligence-
   analysis models.
3. **A classified-research talent pipeline**, recruiting from the same
   pool the civilian AI mission trains, with appropriate clearances.
4. **Strict separation from any commercial cloud dependency**, including
   hardware supply-chain scrutiny, not just network isolation.

Ask: DRDO and the National AI Mission to jointly fund a dedicated,
air-gapped defence AI compute cluster, separate from civilian public
compute infrastructure.
