## The Spark

Green hydrogen — made from renewable electricity and water — is the fuel that
can decarbonise steel, shipping, fertiliser and heavy industry. The countries
with the cheapest solar and wind will make it cheapest. India is one of them.

## The Gap

India has built one of the world's largest renewable-energy fleets, but much
of that power is used domestically. The opportunity is to convert abundant
sunshine into an export commodity and a domestic industrial feedstock —
turning energy leadership into industrial leadership.

## The Precedent

- **National Green Hydrogen Mission** (2023, ~₹19,744 crore) with production
  and export targets.
- **150+ GW** of installed renewable capacity and among the world's lowest
  solar tariffs.
- The **International Solar Alliance**, co-founded by India, already
  coordinates global solar ambition.

## The Proposal

1. **Port-based green-hydrogen hubs** — at Kandla, Paradip, Vizag and other
   ports — co-located with renewables and desalination.
2. **Domestic electrolyser manufacturing** at scale, to cut the cost of the
   core technology.
3. **Export corridors** to Europe and East Asia, plus domestic offtake in
   steel, fertiliser and refining.
4. **A green shipping fuel ambition** — positioning Indian ports as bunkering
   hubs for the coming green-methanol and ammonia trade.

Ask: the Ministry of New & Renewable Energy to publish a hub-level road map
linking renewables, ports and industry.
