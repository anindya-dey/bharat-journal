## Where I saw it

The Svalbard Global Seed Vault exists so that no single disaster can
erase crop diversity — but it is still, in the end, one vault. The
National Knowledge Vault already proposed in this journal backs up
India's manuscripts and data across multiple sites for exactly this
reason; the same single-point-of-failure risk applies just as directly to
living biological material — seeds, livestock genetics, and India's
extraordinary plant and microbial diversity.

## What's missing

India's National Gene Bank already preserves an enormous number of seed
and plant accessions, concentrated substantially at one primary facility.
A disaster at that single site — fire, flood, conflict — would put
decades of irreplaceable genetic material at risk in a way the
manuscripts and data in the knowledge vault already address by design.

## What India already has

- **The National Bureau of Plant Genetic Resources' gene bank**, already
  preserving a vast collection of seed and plant accessions.
- **ICAR's regional research stations**, spread across every major
  climate zone, a ready network of sites to duplicate storage across.
- **The National Knowledge Vault already proposed**, a template for the
  multi-site resilience design this would mirror for biological material.

## The proposal

1. **Full duplication of the gene bank's holdings** across at least
   three climate-zone-separated ICAR stations — Himalayan, peninsular and
   coastal — not just backups of catalogue data.
2. **Extend the principle to livestock genetics**, cryopreserving semen
   and embryo lines from India's indigenous breeds at multiple sites too.
3. **Cold-chain-independent storage research**, since long-term seed
   viability in a power-disrupted scenario is its own engineering
   problem worth solving.
4. **Publish duplication status publicly**, the way other resilience
   programmes report their own redundancy metrics.

Ask: ICAR and the National Bureau of Plant Genetic Resources to fund
full triplication of the national gene bank across climate-zone-separated
sites.
