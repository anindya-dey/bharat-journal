## The Spark

As money, identity and government move online, cyber-security becomes national
infrastructure. India operates some of the largest digital systems in the
world — UPI, Aadhaar, CoWIN — and their resilience is a strategic asset.

## The Gap

India's digital public infrastructure is world-leading, and it deserves
world-leading defence. The opportunity is a unified national cyber-defence
capability that protects citizens, businesses and government systems
proactively — not just responds after an attack.

## The Precedent

- **CERT-In** coordinates national cyber-incident response.
- **I4C** targets cybercrime and financial fraud.
- **UPI's fraud-resilience architecture** shows India can build security into
  public systems from the start.

## The Proposal

1. **A unified cyber-defence grid** — real-time threat intelligence shared
   across CERT-In, banks, telecoms and government systems.
2. **Cyber-resilience standards** for all critical digital public
   infrastructure, with mandatory audits.
3. **A cyber-security talent pipeline** — national cyber-academies and
   certification, addressing the skills shortage.
4. **Public awareness and citizen protection** — anti-fraud tools and rapid
   response built into UPI and banking apps.

Ask: MeitY and CERT-In to publish a national cyber-defence roadmap tied to the
digital public infrastructure it protects.
