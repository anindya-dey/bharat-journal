## The Spark

India has, in a single generation, moved from importing almost everything it
defends itself with to designing and building world-class platforms at home —
a shift few countries have managed. The next milestone is to export at scale.

## The Gap

India's defence industrial base is deepening, but the opportunity is larger:
to become a trusted global supplier of defence hardware, the way it became a
global supplier of software. That means scale, quality and reliability that
world markets will buy.

## The Precedent

- **INS Vikrant** — India's indigenous aircraft carrier, commissioned 2022.
- **Tejas** fighter, **BrahMos**, **Akash** and **Arjun** — indigenous
  platforms in service and export.
- **UP and Tamil Nadu Defence Industrial Corridors** — dedicated manufacturing
  clusters.
- Defence exports have risen sharply, crossing record highs in recent years.

## The Proposal

1. **Scale the two defence corridors** into full industrial ecosystems —
   component suppliers, testing ranges, and skilled labour.
2. **Plug-and-play defence parks** for private and startup participation, with
   clear procurement pathways.
3. **A "design in India, build in India, export from India" incentive** for
   platforms with genuine export potential.
4. **Aim for a step-change in defence exports**, targeting trusted-supplier
   status in friendly markets.

Ask: the Ministry of Defence and the corridors' state governments to publish
a scaled export road map and expand private participation.
