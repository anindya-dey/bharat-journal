## Where I saw it

A single country refines the overwhelming majority of the world's rare
earths and manufactures most of the permanent magnets made from them —
the components inside EV motors, wind-turbine generators and precision-
guided weapons. Export restrictions from that one source rattled
automakers and defence planners elsewhere badly enough that India has now
moved, with real money, to stop depending on it.

## What's missing

India held meaningful rare-earth reserves but almost no domestic refining
or magnet-manufacturing capacity — until a funded scheme and dedicated
mining corridors were approved to close exactly that gap. This sits
squarely inside the broader strategic-resource-independence doctrine
already proposed in this journal; what's left is seeing the new scheme
through from approval to actual production.

## What India already has

- **A ₹7,280 crore Scheme to Promote Manufacturing of Sintered Rare Earth
  Permanent Magnets**, approved by the Union Cabinet in November 2025, to
  build 6,000 metric tonnes per annum of integrated capacity from
  rare-earth oxides to finished magnets.
- **Dedicated Rare Earth Corridors announced across Odisha, Kerala,
  Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu**, to secure the upstream mining this
  scheme depends on.
- **IREL (India Rare Earths Limited)**, a public-sector company already
  mining and partially processing rare-earth minerals, the base the new
  scheme builds on.
- **A fast-growing EV and wind-turbine manufacturing base**, the exact
  domestic customer this supply chain would serve.

## The ask

1. **Move the corridors from announcement to producing ore** on a public
   timeline, since the magnet scheme's output target depends entirely on
   upstream supply arriving on schedule.
2. **Publish production milestones** for the 6,000 MTPA target, the way
   other national missions report progress against their own numbers.
3. **Diversify import partnerships** for any shortfall during ramp-up,
   following the minimum-two-source doctrine already proposed for
   strategic inputs generally.
4. **Prioritise defence and EV-motor-grade magnets first** as the scheme
   scales, the two applications with the clearest strategic urgency.

Ask: the Ministry of Mines and Ministry of Heavy Industries to publish
public production milestones linking the rare-earth corridors to the
REPM scheme's 6,000 MTPA target.
