## Where I saw it

Israel, the US and New Zealand each run dairy herds with yield per animal
several times India's national average, achieved mainly through sexed
semen, genomic selection and embryo transfer rather than larger herds.
India's dairy sector, built on the back of the White Revolution, has never
had the same systematic genetic push.

## What's missing

India is the world's largest milk producer by volume, built on millions
of smallholder animals rather than a few large, highly productive herds.
The opportunity is not to change that structure, but to raise the
productivity of each animal within it, through the genetic tools other
dairy leaders already use routinely.

## What India already has

- **NDDB (National Dairy Development Board)**, already running breeding
  and genomic-selection programmes at a national scale.
- **India's indigenous cattle breeds**, including hardy, heat-tolerant
  genetics few other dairy-exporting countries have access to.
- **The cooperative dairy network** built by the White Revolution, a
  distribution and farmer-engagement structure ready to deliver improved
  genetics to millions of smallholders directly.

## The proposal

1. **Sexed semen and embryo-transfer access for every dairy cooperative
   member**, not just the largest commercial operations.
2. **A genomic-selection breeding programme** for India's own indigenous
   breeds, preserving their heat tolerance while raising yield.
3. **Veterinary and AI (artificial insemination) technician training**
   tied to the cooperative network, so the genetics reach the herd, not
   just the breeding centre.
4. **Publish a national yield-per-animal target**, tracked the way milk
   production totals already are.

Ask: NDDB and the Department of Animal Husbandry & Dairying to publish a
national dairy-genomics scale-up plan through the cooperative network.
