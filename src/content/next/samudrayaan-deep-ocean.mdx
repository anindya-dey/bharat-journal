## Where I saw it

More than 80% of the ocean is unmapped and unexplored. The deep sea holds
mineral resources, extraordinary biodiversity, and climate clues — and the
nations that map it will shape the rules for a vast new frontier.

## What's missing

India has a long coastline, island territories, and a growing marine economy.
The opportunity is to build a genuine deep-sea capability — crewed
submersibles, seabed mapping, and ocean science — and to have a voice in how
the deep ocean is governed.

## What India already has

- **Deep Ocean Mission** — India's programme for deep-sea exploration,
  including the **Samudrayaan** crewed submersible (Matsya-6000).
- **India's Antarctic and Arctic stations** show a sustained polar-science
  capability.
- **Blue Economy** initiatives across the Ministry of Earth Sciences.

## The ask

1. **Deliver Samudrayaan** — a crewed submersible able to reach 6,000 metres,
   joining a small club of deep-sea nations.
2. **Map India's exclusive economic zone** — a full seabed survey to understand
   resources, hazards and ecosystems.
3. **Deep-sea mineral and biodiversity science** — with strict environmental
   safeguards and international cooperation.
4. **A marine-technology industry** — submersibles, sensors, and underwater
   robotics as an Indian export capability.

Ask: the Ministry of Earth Sciences to expand the Deep Ocean Mission with a published mapping and technology roadmap.
