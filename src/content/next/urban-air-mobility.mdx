## Where I saw it

Joby, Archer and EHang are flight-testing electric air taxis; Dubai, Singapore
and São Paulo are preparing launch corridors. For cities where the average
commute crawls, the sky is an unused lane.

## What's missing

India has the demand case almost no one else has: enormous metros, brutal
congestion, and a booming aviation market. What's missing is the enabling
layer — airspace rules for low-altitude flight, certified vertiports, and a
domestic eVTOL industry.

## What India already has

- **Drone rules 2021** opened Indian airspace to unmanned flight and created a
  domestic drone industry almost overnight.
- **[UDAN](https://www.civilaviation.gov.in/)** showed India can build a regional-aviation network from scratch.
- Indian startups and HAL are already exploring eVTOL designs.

## The proposal

1. **A low-altitude airspace framework** — digital corridors, traffic
   management, and certification standards for eVTOL aircraft.
2. **Vertiports at transit hubs** — rooftop and station-adjacent pads at metro
   termini, airports and business districts, planned into new cities.
3. **An eVTOL manufacturing incentive** — position India as a builder, not just
   a buyer, of the next aviation segment.
4. **Start with emergency and premium use** — medical organ transfer, disaster
   response, and airport transfers — before scaling to commuting.

Ask: the Ministry of Civil Aviation and DGCA to publish a national urban-air-
mobility road map.
