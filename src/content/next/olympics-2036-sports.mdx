## Where I saw it

Hosting the Olympics transforms a country's relationship with sport. Beijing,
London and Tokyo used the Games to build infrastructure, national pride and a
generation of athletes. India — young, ambitious, and already a major sporting
market — is a natural next host.

## What's missing

India's sporting success has been rising fast, but the base is still thin:
talent is found late, facilities are uneven, and many sports lack a
professional pathway. The opportunity is to use an Olympic bid as the reason
to build the base — not just the venues.

## What India already has

- **Asian Games 2023** — India's largest-ever medal haul.
- **Khelo India** — a grassroots talent pipeline already finding athletes
  from small towns.
- **CWG 2010 Delhi** proved India can stage a major multi-sport event and
  left lasting venues.

## The proposal

1. **Bid for the 2036 Summer Olympics**, with a credible, sustainable plan
   that reuses and upgrades existing venues.
2. **Build a district-level sports base** — a playable facility and a coach in
   every district, so talent is found at ten, not twenty.
3. **A professional pathway per sport** — leagues, scholarships and coaching
   tied to the Olympic cycle.
4. **Legacy by design** — every venue built for the Games doubles as public
   sports infrastructure afterwards.

Ask: the Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports and the IOA to build the bid
around a nationwide talent pipeline, not just host-city venues.
