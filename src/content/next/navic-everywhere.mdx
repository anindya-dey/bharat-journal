## Where I saw it

The US (GPS), Russia (GLONASS), the EU (Galileo) and China (BeiDou) all run
global satellite-navigation constellations — a strategic asset no country
wants to depend on another for. India built its own, NavIC, and has now
begun mandating its use in the one device almost every citizen carries.

## What's missing

NavIC gives India sovereign positioning that cannot be switched off by
another country — valuable for aviation, shipping, disaster response and
defence. The smartphone mandate is real and phased in; what's still
missing is vehicles, critical infrastructure timing, and the constellation's
limited regional footprint, which stops well short of GPS's global reach.

## What India already has

- **A government mandate requiring NavIC support in smartphones**, phased
  in from January 2025 for 5G devices and extending to other handsets
  afterwards.
- **NavIC (formerly IRNSS)** — India's own regional satellite-navigation
  constellation, operational since 2018.
- **[GAGAN](https://www.aai.aero/)**, India's satellite-based augmentation system, already improving
  GPS accuracy for aviation.
- A growing number of **chipset makers building NavIC support** to meet
  the mandate.

## The ask

1. **Complete the phased smartphone rollout** to every handset, not just
   5G devices, and verify real-world compliance, not just chipset
   specifications.
2. **Extend the mandate to vehicles**, where NavIC's safety and
   positioning case is at least as strong as for phones.
3. **NavIC for critical infrastructure timing** — railways, power-grid
   synchronisation and financial-system timestamps — so India isn't
   dependent on a foreign signal for systems that must never go dark.
4. **Extend NavIC's footprint** toward full regional and, eventually,
   global coverage, with more satellites in the constellation.

Ask: ISRO, DoT and MeitY to publish compliance data on the smartphone
mandate and extend it to vehicles and critical infrastructure.
