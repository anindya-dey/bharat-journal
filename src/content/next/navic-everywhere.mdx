## Where I saw it

The US (GPS), Russia (GLONASS), the EU (Galileo) and China (BeiDou) all run
global satellite-navigation constellations — a strategic asset no country
wants to depend on another for. India built its own, NavIC, and almost no
phone or car in India actually uses it by default.

## What's missing

NavIC gives India sovereign positioning that cannot be switched off by
another country — valuable for aviation, shipping, disaster response and
defence. But it currently covers India and a modest radius beyond, and
device support is patchy even within that footprint. The opportunity is to
finish the job: make it the default, then extend its reach.

## What India already has

- **NavIC (formerly IRNSS)** — India's own regional satellite-navigation
  constellation, operational since 2018.
- **[GAGAN](https://www.aai.aero/)** — India's satellite-based augmentation system, already improving
  GPS accuracy for aviation.
- A growing number of **chipsets with NavIC support**, and mandates for
  NavIC in some government and public-safety devices.

## The proposal

1. **Mandate NavIC chipsets** in all new smartphones and vehicles sold in
   India, alongside GPS, not instead of it.
2. **Extend NavIC's footprint** toward full regional and, eventually, global
   coverage, with more satellites in the constellation.
3. **NavIC for critical infrastructure** — railways, power grid timing, and
   financial-system timestamps, so India isn't dependent on a foreign
   signal for systems that must never go dark.
4. **Open the signal for innovation** — precision agriculture, logistics and
   disaster response apps built natively on NavIC.

Ask: ISRO and the Department of Telecommunications to publish a NavIC
adoption mandate and a constellation-expansion roadmap.
