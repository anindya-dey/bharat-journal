## Where I saw it

Planned greenfield cities like South Korea's Songdo and Saudi Arabia's
NEOM have each tried to build "the city of the future" around one or two
headline technologies — smart sensors, or a single novel urban form. None
has yet combined every advanced-mobility layer — hyperloop, air-taxi
corridors, underground rail and freight, and net-zero energy — into one
city's masterplan from the start, rather than retrofitting pieces onto an
existing one.

## What's missing

This journal already carries separate proposals for hyperloop transit,
urban air mobility, net-zero cities, satellite capitals and common
underground utility ducts. Each stands on its own merits, but none of
them is designed as one integrated system. A genuinely new greenfield city
is the only place all of them can be built together, layered from the
ground up rather than bolted on later.

## What India already has

- **GIFT City and Amaravati**, proof India already plans and builds
  greenfield cities from scratch.
- **Every underlying technology already proposed separately** in this
  journal — hyperloop, air mobility, net-zero building codes, common
  utility ducts and satellite-city design.
- **A genuine need for new urban capacity**, since India will build more
  urban space in the coming decades than it has built in its entire
  history so far.

## The proposal

1. **Pick one new greenfield city** as the explicit integration point for
   every advanced-mobility proposal already on the table, rather than
   piloting each separately in different cities.
2. **Design the underground layer first** — utility ducts, rail and
   freight tunnels — since retrofitting underground infrastructure later
   is the single costliest mistake other "future cities" have made.
3. **Reserve air-taxi and hyperloop corridors in the masterplan from day
   one**, even if built in later phases, so land and airspace aren't
   contested after the fact.
4. **Publish it as a living template**, not a one-off showcase, so
   lessons transfer to the next greenfield city, not just this one.

Ask: NITI Aayog and the Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs to designate
one planned greenfield city as the integration pilot for every advanced-
mobility proposal already in motion.
