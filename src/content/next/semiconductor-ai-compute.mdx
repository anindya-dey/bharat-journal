## The Spark

Every major economy has concluded the same thing: chips are strategic. The US
CHIPS Act, Taiwan's foundries, and the EU's chip push all reflect it. India
has the rarer asset — a deep reservoir of chip *design* talent inside the
world's largest semiconductor companies — and has now begun to build the
fabrication side too.

## The Gap

India has long designed chips and imported them. The opportunity is to close
the loop: fabs, packaging, and the AI compute that chips power, built on
Indian soil.

## The Precedent

- **India Semiconductor Mission** (2021, ~US$10 bn) is funding fabs, ATMP and
  display units.
- **Micron's ATMP plant** (Sanand), the **Tata–PSMC fab** (Dholera) and
  **CG Power, Kaynes** packaging units are the first bricks.
- The **Design Linked Incentive (DLI)** scheme backs fabless Indian startups.
- The **National AI Mission** has begun procuring public GPU capacity.

## The Proposal

1. **Scale the fab and ATMP build-out** from pilot plants to volume, with a
   clear road map to leading-edge nodes over time.
2. **Grow the fabless ecosystem** — more DLI-style support, shared EDA tools,
   and design parks next to the fabs.
3. **Build public AI compute** as infrastructure: GPU clusters available to
   startups, researchers and government at cost, on the data-centre grid.
4. **Talent pipeline** — semiconductor and AI curricula in every major
   engineering cluster, tied to the fabs.

Ask: continue and expand the Semiconductor Mission and National AI Mission,
with a published target for domestic AI compute capacity.
