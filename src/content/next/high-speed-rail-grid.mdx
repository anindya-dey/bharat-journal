## Where I saw it

High-speed rail has reshaped economies from Japan to China to Europe, turning
cities hours apart into a single labour and travel market. India's first
corridor is rising in Gujarat and Maharashtra, and the first regional rapid
transit line is already running near Delhi.

## What's missing

India's trunk rail network is now almost fully electrified — the foundation is
ready. The opportunity is speed: linking the major metros with high-speed rail
so that Delhi–Mumbai, Mumbai–Chennai and the rest become day trips, not
overnight journeys.

## What India already has

- **Mumbai–Ahmedabad High-Speed Rail** (508 km, under construction, Japanese
  collaboration).
- **Namo Bharat RRTS** — the Delhi–Meerut regional rapid transit, operational
  since 2023, running at 160 km/h.
- **Vande Bharat** trainsets have brought semi-high-speed service to much of
  the network.
- **~99% route electrification** (see Now) means the network is ready.

## The ask

1. **Complete the Mumbai–Ahmedabad corridor** and use it as the template for
   the rest.
2. **Build the Diamond Quadrilateral** — Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai and Kolkata
   linked by high-speed rail, with the diagonals to follow.
3. **Indigenous trainsets and track** — a domestic high-speed manufacturing
   base, so the programme compounds into an industry.
4. **Integrate with metro and RRTS** at each end, so high-speed rail is the
   spine of a seamless national network.
