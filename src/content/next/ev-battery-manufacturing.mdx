## Where I saw it

The electric transition is the biggest reshaping of the auto industry in a
century, and batteries are its strategic core. China built dominance in
batteries deliberately; the US and EU are now spending heavily to catch up.
India — with a huge home market and cheap renewable power — can build its own.

## What's missing

India imports most of its lithium-ion cells and critical minerals. The
opportunity is to localise the supply chain — cells, packs, motors and
recycling — and to power it with India's renewable grid, so the EV transition
compounds the energy transition.

## What India already has

- **PLI schemes** for auto and advanced-chemistry cells are already funding
  domestic battery manufacturing.
- **India's two- and three-wheeler market** is the world's largest and is
  electrifying fast — a perfect launch market.
- **Critical-minerals partnerships** with resource-rich countries are being
  negotiated.

## The proposal

1. **Gigafactories at scale** — domestic lithium-ion cell manufacturing under
   the PLI/ACC schemes, moving from pilot to volume.
2. **Secure the critical minerals** — overseas partnerships plus domestic
   exploration and, crucially, **battery recycling** to recover materials.
3. **Build the full stack** — motors, power electronics, and charging
   infrastructure, not just assembly.
4. **Electrify public and freight fleets first** — buses, three-wheelers and
   commercial vehicles, where the economics already work.

Ask: the Ministry of Heavy Industries and MeitY to publish a full-stack
EV-and-battery roadmap with a domestic-content target.
